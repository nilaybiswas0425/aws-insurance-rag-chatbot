# ABC Insurance – Home Insurance Policy

## Policy Details

Policy Number: HOME-2026-001
Policy Type: Home Insurance
Coverage Amount: CAD 500,000
Annual Premium: CAD 1,250
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

## Property Coverage

The policy covers accidental physical damage to the insured residential property caused by fire, lightning, windstorms and certain water damage events, subject to policy exclusions and limits.

The maximum property coverage is CAD 500,000.

## Personal Belongings

Personal belongings inside the insured property are covered up to CAD 100,000.

High-value items may have individual coverage limits.

## Deductible

The standard deductible is CAD 1,000 per covered claim.

Water damage claims may have a separate deductible depending on the coverage selected.

## Claims Process

Policyholders must report an incident to ABC Insurance as soon as reasonably possible.

The policyholder must provide supporting documents, photographs and other relevant information required for claim assessment.

## Important Exclusions

The policy does not cover:
- Intentional damage caused by the policyholder.
- Normal wear and tear.
- Damage resulting from poor maintenance.
- Flood damage unless specific flood coverage has been purchased.
